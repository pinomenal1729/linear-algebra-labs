12 places matrices run the show

🎮GRAPHICS

Game Engines & 3D Worlds

Every vertex on your screen is a vector pushed through a chain of matrices — model, view, projection — to land exactly where the camera sees it. Rotate the camera? That's another matrix.

p′ = M · V · P · p
🎬GRAPHICS

VFX & Animation Films

CG characters bend with blend shapes, light bounces with normal matrices, and every frame is a projection. The "wow" in blockbuster movies is linear algebra on a budget.

n̂ = M · n / ‖M · n‖
🔍WEB

Search Engines (PageRank)

Google's original magic: rank every page as the eigenvector of a giant link matrix. The internet is a matrix, and the top result is an eigenvalue story.

P · r = 1 · r

🧠AI & ML

Machine Learning

Data points are vectors, models are linear maps, and "learning" is walking downhill on a loss surface. Nearly every neural network starts as a giant matrix multiply.

ŷ = X · θ

📷AI & ML

Computer Vision

An image is a matrix of numbers. Face recognition compresses faces with PCA, filters are matrix convolutions, and self-driving cars read the road as one giant matrix.

A ∈ ℝᵐˣⁿ

🦾ENGINEERING

Robotics

Every joint is a rotation matrix; the arm's reach is a chain of them. The Jacobian matrix tells the robot which motors to move so the hand lands where you want.

q̇ = J⁺ · ẋ
🛰️ENGINEERING

GPS & Navigation

Your phone solves a least-squares problem every time you check the map: too many noisy satellite signals, one best answer. The formula is straight out of the lab.

x̂ = (AᵀA)⁻¹Aᵀb
🎧SIGNAL

Music & Audio

Spotify's audio analysis, the MP3 in your library, noise-cancelling headphones — all built on the Fourier transform, which is just a change of basis into the frequency world.

X = W · x
🧬SCIENCE

Biology & Medicine

Gene-expression data is a giant matrix; SVD and PCA find the patterns that separate healthy from diseased. MRI machines literally reconstruct images by solving matrix equations.

A = U · Σ · Vᵀ
💰FINANCE

Finance & Portfolios

Risk is a covariance matrix. Quants balance hundreds of assets by diagonalizing that matrix — eigenvectors become "risk factors" and eigenvalues become their strength.

σ² = wᵀ · Σ · w
🍿TECH

Recommendation Engines

Netflix's famous problem: a users × movies matrix that is almost all empty. Factorize it (A ≈ U·Vᵀ) and the gaps fill themselves with your next favorite show.

A ≈ U · Vᵀ

⚛️FUTURE

Quantum Computing

A quantum state is a vector; every quantum gate is a unitary matrix; measuring is a projection. The whole field is linear algebra wearing a physics costume.

|ψ′⟩ = U · |ψ⟩
