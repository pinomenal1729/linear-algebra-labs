One course. Four labs. All careers.

Every career above is built on the same three pillars — the exact things you'll build by hand in the labs.

PILLAR 01 🧭

Vectors are data

A photo, a song, a user, a webpage — anything can be a list of numbers in space. Distance, angle and direction become tools, not metaphors.

x = (x₁, x₂, …, xₙ)
PILLAR 02 🔄

Matrices are transformations

Rotating a camera, training a model, ranking a web — all of it is multiplying by the right matrix. Learn one operation, understand a thousand systems.

p′ = A · p
PILLAR 03 💎

Eigenvalues are structure

Some directions barely change when a matrix acts on them. Those are the stable directions — of the web, of risk, of data. Find them, and you've found the truth.

A · v = λ · v
