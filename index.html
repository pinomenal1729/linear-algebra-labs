The learning architecture

Question → data → mathematics → decision

The same disciplined workflow appears in computer vision, search, machine learning and recommendation systems.

01

Observe

Begin with a real system: an image, a network, a dataset or your viewing preferences.

02

Represent

Convert the system into vectors, matrices, coordinates and measurable features.

03

Compute

04

The hidden connection

Modern technology is built by choosing the right mathematical representation.

An image becomes a matrix. A website becomes a graph. Training becomes motion on a surface. Taste becomes a vector. Once the representation is correct, the computation becomes visible.
