The local linear map

For a small joint change, the Jacobian converts joint motion into hand motion:

Δp ≈ J Δθ

Your compensated motion kept the hand fixed:

J Δθ ≈ 0
Δθ ∈ Null(J)

All configurations reaching the target

One configuration reaches the hand target. Null-space motion generates other configurations with the same output:

θ = θₚ + t n

J n = 0

The robot uses this hidden freedom to avoid obstacles without abandoning its task.

Why did three joints create hidden freedom?

The hand position has two coordinates, but the arm has three controllable joint angles. One internal degree of freedom remains.

What does the output “not see”?

It does not see joint changes that cancel one another and produce zero net hand displacement.

What role does rank play?

Rank counts the independently controllable hand-motion directions. Nullity counts the remaining invisible joint-motion directions.
