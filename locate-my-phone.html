WHY A CIRCLE?

One measurement leaves infinitely many locations.

When data disagrees

The least-squares estimate satisfies:

AᵀA x̂ = Aᵀb

Rank tells us whether the towers provide enough independent information for a unique position.

Why was one tower insufficient?

It gave distance but no direction, leaving an entire ring.

Why were two towers still ambiguous?

Two rings generally intersect at two points.

What did Tower C contribute?

A new independent constraint that selected one candidate.

Accuracy note: This models terrestrial network trilateration. Satellite GPS applies the distance-from-known-points idea in 3D and usually needs at least four satellites because it must also estimate receiver clock error.
